# corpus/foundation/

The framework's starting assumptions, written down so they can be argued with.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `axioms.md` | The core axiom, that reality can be read as recursive symbolic transformation, and the formal postulates that give it a notation (symbolic state, recursive operator, and so on). |
| `mathematical-foundation.md` | The notation in detail: a symbol as a multidimensional state, symbolic transformation, attractors, termination, and what is measured and how. |

**How to read them.** The core axiom is marked by its own text as register C: a chosen lens that cannot be proven
or disproven, not a claim about what is. The postulates are notation for it. Nothing here is tested, and nothing
here should be cited as VERIFIED. Where the corpus does test something formal that comes from these documents, the
claim and its test are in [`../ledgers/`](../ledgers/README.md).

The three registers (A: traditional, B: formal and checkable, C: metaphysical) are explained in
[`../docs/PHILOSOPHICAL_AND_ENGINEERING_BOUNDARIES.md`](../docs/PHILOSOPHICAL_AND_ENGINEERING_BOUNDARIES.md).
