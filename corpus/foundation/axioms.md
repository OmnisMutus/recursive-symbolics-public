# Axioms

> **The Core Axiom (a chosen reading, Register C).** Read through this framework, reality is neither fundamentally
> matter nor mind, but recursive symbolic transformation. Matter, mind, mathematics and language are then four forms
> of one recursive process. This cannot be proven or disproven. It stands in the same register as the Kabbalistic
> reading: a lens the framework uses, not a claim about what is.
>
> ### Formal Postulates (notation)
> 1. **Symbolic State**: Any system of interest (a mind, a conversation, a project) can be written as a symbolic
>    state vector $\sigma$, holding what is representable about that system at time $t$.
> 2. **Recursive Operator**: A change in the system is written as an operator $O$ applied to $\sigma$, giving a new
>    state: $\sigma' = O(\sigma)$. In this notation, consciousness is modeled as the selecting and applying of
>    operators.
> 3. **Attractor**: Applying $O$ again and again defines a dynamical system. In this notation, a stable moment of
>    experience is modeled as an attractor of that system. Whether a given $O$ has an attractor, and of what kind, is
>    computation (Register B). Whether such an attractor *is* consciousness is Register C.

## The Reading Against Its Alternatives

Philosophy has long divided between materialism (matter is fundamental, mind emerges from it) and idealism (mind is
fundamental, matter is its manifestation). This framework's reading takes neither side: it treats the process of
recursive symbolic transformation as the common term. In this reading the universe is computational, not in the sense
of a digital computer, but as a dynamical system of symbols rewriting themselves by recursive rules.

## Four Forms of One Process

In this reading, matter, mind, mathematics and language differ in level of abstraction, structural complexity and
rate of transformation, not in kind:

*   **Matter**: read as highly stable, low-level symbolic configurations governed by strict, repetitive rules
    (physical laws).
*   **Mind**: read as a highly dynamic, self-referential recursive process on abstract symbols, capable of rapid
    transformation and reflection on itself.
*   **Mathematics**: read as the formalization of the recursive rules themselves, apart from any material or mental
    instance.
*   **Language**: read as the symbolic interface through which minds exchange and synchronize their states.

## Examples Read as Recursive Structures

Each of these can be read as a recursive information structure. The reading is the framework's; the facts in each
line are ordinary science or practice:

1.  **An atom**: interacting particles whose relations form a stable configuration that keeps its identity over time.
2.  **A gene**: a sequence of nucleotides encoding proteins, which in turn regulate other genes, forming feedback loops.
3.  **A neuron**: a cell that integrates inputs from other neurons and emits a new signal, the unit of neural networks.
4.  **A sentence**: words governed by a recursive grammar, whose meaning is built from the meanings of its parts.
5.  **A mathematical proof**: deductions from axioms, applying rules of inference again and again to reach a conclusion.
6.  **A ritual**: a structured sequence of actions, utterances and symbols meant to induce a state of consciousness
    in its participants.
7.  **A civilization**: people, institutions and artifacts that interact, adapt and change over historical time.

## Working Principle: Everything Read as a Recursive Symbol

> **Everything can be read as a recursive symbol.**

This is the framework's working principle, not a finding. Any entity, process or concept can be written as a symbol
(a distinct informational state) undergoing recursive transformation (interacting with other symbols or with itself to
produce new states). Writing them all this way gives one method for comparing physical systems, organisms, cognitive
processes and formal systems. What the comparison means is the reader's to supply.
